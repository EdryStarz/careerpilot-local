## Short English post

CareerPilot Local is a public-preview HH.ru job-search assistant with Ollama and Telegram review. Bring your own profile and model, start in dry-run, and review applications before enabling submissions. Open source, with explicit limits: no universal ATS support or CAPTCHA bypass. Looking for reproducible, redacted bug reports and contributions.

https://github.com/EdryStarz/careerpilot-local

## Demo checklist

1. Use a fictional candidate and a disposable test bot.
2. Show setup, dry-run and a generated preview, not an unverified real submission.
3. Hide tokens, account identifiers, browser cookies and real employer messages.
4. Explain local hardware requirements and where data actually goes.
5. Link the README and ask for feedback. Do not claim guaranteed jobs, popularity or statistics you did not measure.
