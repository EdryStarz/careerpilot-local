## Short English post

CareerPilot Local is a local-first, multi-source career assistant. Bring your own CV, Ollama model and Telegram account. Search public boards and channels, chat naturally with a private bot, and explicitly opt into supported form/Telegram applications after a dry run. Confirmed-result notifications, a private ledger and conservative unknown-result handling. Open-source beta with clear limits: not every ATS form is supported; no paid bids or CAPTCHA bypass. Looking for reproducible, redacted bug reports and contributions.

https://github.com/EdryStarz/careerpilot-local

## Demo checklist

1. Use a fictional candidate and a disposable test bot.
2. Show setup, dry-run and an intercepted local form fixture, not an unverified real submission.
3. Hide tokens, account identifiers, browser cookies and real employer messages.
4. Explain local hardware requirements and where data actually goes.
5. Link the README and ask for feedback. Do not claim guaranteed jobs, popularity or statistics you did not measure.
